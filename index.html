Settings

Finnhub WebSocket trades drive the current US price and are classified into pre-market, regular-session, and post-market values as they arrive. During pre/post-market, Fast Snapshot Boost also checks the currently analyzed ticker about once per second (quota-safe) and round-robins other live tickers when no analysis is open. Session values show their age so stale extended-hours prints are obvious. The free Finnhub feed is still last-trade based; brokerage-style continuously moving bid/ask marks require a bid/ask-capable data entitlement. Twelve Data is reserved for chart analysis / REST fallback. Purchased positions use their own profit goal and maximum recovery-share cap. The final analyzer forecasts a user-selected horizon from historical analogs plus live candles/order flow, then confirms with multi-timeframe trend, EMA/SMA, ADX/DMI, RSI, ATR, VWAP, Ichimoku, Volume Profile, FVG, BOS/CHoCH, liquidity sweeps, opening-range and news context. A per-ticker adaptive learning layer records forecast outcomes, learns stock-specific bias/direction calibration, and is allowed to influence forecasts only as it accumulates evidence. It can hold a winner across sessions, tighten a dynamic sell guard, or show when a technically supported recovery buy is reasonable. All signals stay inside the app; no browser notifications or automatic pop-up alerts are used.

Analyze New Stock

This creates a fresh-entry trade idea in Considering, even if you already own the same ticker. The desired profit is NET: the analyzer adds the configured buy and sell commissions (default $6.95 + $6.95) before sizing the trade. It does not use your existing cost basis, change portfolio totals, or record a purchase.
