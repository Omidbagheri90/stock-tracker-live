Settings

Global intelligence is low-frequency; live entry timing remains second-by-second.
Portfolio and Considering tickers are always included automatically.
The primary free U.S. live feed is Yahoo Finance WebSocket and requires no API key. It streams price updates and, when supplied by Yahoo, bid/ask plus pre-market / regular / post-market session labels. Finnhub is retained only as an optional fallback. Twelve Data is reserved for historical 5-minute/daily candles and deeper strategy calculations. The trading engine now has two loops: a fast loop evaluates the live tape every second to detect an active dip, falling knife, reversal, feasible target and 1/2/3+ share sizing; a slower model loop refreshes multi-timeframe structure, FVG, VWAP, Ichimoku, Volume Profile, ADX/DMI, historical analogs, adaptive learning and news. A requested profit is never forced: the app chooses a chart-supported target first, subtracts both commissions, and says whether the requested net amount is reachable or what lower/higher amount is more feasible. A per-stock online ML layer evaluates live dip/reversal setups second by second, learns from false and successful turns, and tightens or relaxes confirmation for similar future setups. Yahoo/yfinance-style public data is for personal-use decision support and can occasionally be unavailable; manual price mode remains the fallback. All signals stay inside the app; no browser notification pop-ups are used.
